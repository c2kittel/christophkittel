Ahead of our departure on 19 November, the MOAT field campaign has caught the attention of the Belgian press. A joint press release from the ULB and the VUB announced that a team of researchers will spend two months on the Roi Baudouin Ice Shelf in Antarctica to study ice melt and its contribution to sea level rise.
